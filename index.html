Gesture Synth Guide

✋ Left Hand

Controls the chord.

  • 1 Finger → I
  • 2 Fingers → II
  • 3 Fingers → III
  • 4 Fingers → IV
  • 5 Fingers → V
  • Index + Pinky → VI
  • Index + Pinky + Thumb → VII

🤚 Right Hand

Controls voicing, volume and filter.

  • Raise Hand → Increase Volume
  • Lower Hand → Reduce Volume
  • Tilt Hand → Filter Sweep
  • Thumb → Octave Down

Tips

Use a bright room and keep both hands inside the camera view for the best tracking performance.
